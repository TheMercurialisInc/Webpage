± Welcome to Mercurialis Inc.

Founded October 28, 2002 during the beta of EVE Online, Mercurialis Inc. (MINC) is the oldest active corporation in New Eden. For more than twenty years we've fought through every era of the game, from the early nullsec wars, through the rise of supercapital warfare, and into the modern age of titan-fleet brawls. Our pilots have been on grid for some of EVE's defining battles: Asakai, B-R5RB, the Casino War, X47L-Q, FWST-8, and M2-XFE. From ten-thousand-pilot coalition fights to small-gang roams, we've always found a place on the field.

Beyond combat, MINC is a home for exploration, industry, capital production, and the friendships that come with two decades of flying together. Our members span the globe, veterans with a decade-plus in the game alongside new pilots writing their first chapters. More than twenty years on, we're still here because generations of pilots chose to build something together. And there's still history to be written.

± Join Mercurialis Inc.

We're recruiting across all time zones. You don't need to be a veteran or know every mechanic in EVE. You need a good attitude, respect for others, and a willingness to get in fleet.

MINC is a nullsec corporation built around experienced veterans, new pilots, and the community that connects them. Our members fight alliance wars, run small-gang PvP, build the industry that keeps operations moving, and explore the far corners of New Eden. Most importantly, we're a group of people who log in because there's someone to fly with.

The universe has changed a lot over the years, and modern EVE is a lot to catch up on. Our members help new and returning pilots with ship fitting, skill planning, ISK generation, and nullsec survival. What we ask in return is participation. We value pilots who show up over names that just fill a roster.

What you can expect

  • More than two decades of history and a future still being written
  • Active pilots across time zones with plenty of opportunities to fly together
  • Experienced members happy to help you get up to speed on modern EVE
  • The infrastructure to operate in nullsec: logistics, industry support, and corp programs
  • A community that values participation over roster count

± How to Join

  1. Join our in-game channel: MINC BAR
  2. Introduce yourself to a recruiter or director.
  3. Have a conversation with us and learn more about the corp.
  4. Authenticate through SeAT and complete onboarding.
  5. Wait for the background check. No peeking.

Recruitment fees remain at 0 ISK. Bribes are still accepted and appreciated.

± Requirements

Membership. MINC has survived more than twenty years because we take trust, respect, and community seriously. The CEO and directorate reserve the right to remove members if that trust is violated.

Attitude. MINC is a multicultural, inclusive corp. Bigotry, harassment, and deliberate toxicity are not accepted.

Activity. We don't require 24/7 play. Real life comes first, but stay reasonably active and let us know if you'll be away for a while.

Fleet participation. Mandatory only for strategic alliance operations. Regular fleets are voluntary, but the more you show up, the more you'll get out of it.

Security. Members use SeAT authentication, provide the required ESI access, and join the corp Discord.

You'll also want an Omega account, and capital-capable pilots, FC experience, industry skills, and a PvP killboard history are all nice to have, none required.
